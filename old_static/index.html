About Me

Combining Nursing Excellence with Workplace Safety

As the founder and Managing Director of Working for Health Limited, I wear the hats of both an Occupational Health Nurse and a Health and Safety Consultant. I bring a multi-dimensional approach to health and safety management, with a core commitment to ensuring that 'health' is never treated as safety's poor cousin.

I enjoy empowering individuals and businesses to look after their own health and safety. Furthermore, I am deeply motivated by mentoring, supporting, and developing other health and safety practitioners into excellence. I am an avid learner, ensuring that my team and I stay at the forefront of emerging research and industry best practices.
